FAQ

Is Reading Log a subscription?

No. Reading Log is free to try for 14 days with full feature access. After that, it's a one-time $3.99 purchase — not a recurring subscription. There's no banking built in; it's a reading log only.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Reading Log → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

What happens when the trial ends?

Adding or editing log entries requires the one-time unlock. Viewing Calendar, History, and Weekly Summary is not gated.

Does my data sync between devices, or to the cloud?

No. Reading Log v1 is local-first: kid profiles and log entries stay on the iPhone you're using. There is no account, no server, and no iCloud sync in v1.

How many kids can I add?

Up to 4 kid profiles per device.

What does a log entry include?

Book title, minutes or pages (you pick a default unit per kid), date, and an optional note.

Will my trial reset if I reinstall?

Your trial start date is stored on-device. Reinstalling on the same device generally preserves your trial window. A new device starts a new trial period.

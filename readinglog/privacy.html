Reading Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Reading Log: Nightly Minutes (“the app” or “Reading Log”) is published by Chad Kanera (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. Reading Log is a parent-operated utility. It is local-first: it works on your device with no account and no servers we operate. There is no iCloud sync in v1.

Summary

  • We do not operate user accounts or a server. There is nowhere for your family's data to be sent.
  • We do not sell personal data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Kid profiles and reading log entries stay on your device. v1 does not use iCloud or any other cloud sync.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • Kid profiles — a name, an avatar (an SF Symbol and color), preferred unit (minutes or pages), and an optional nightly goal.
  • Reading log entries — book title, minutes or pages, date, and an optional note.
  • Settings — trial/unlock state, daily reminder time, and appearance preference.

Kid profiles, reading entries, and settings live on your device (SwiftData). A small widget snapshot — the selected kid's name, streak, and whether tonight is logged — is stored in Apple's shared App Group container (group.com.shiftcrew.readinglog) so the Home Screen widget can display it.

We do not transmit any of this data to our own servers, because we don't operate any.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device using Apple's standard notification APIs. No push notification server or third-party service is involved.

Purchases

Reading Log offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may collect information according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The app stores a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support).
  • Precise location.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone input, or your photo library.
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

Children's privacy

Reading Log is a parent-operated utility for tracking K–5 nightly reading homework on the parent's own device. It is not Made for Kids and is not directed at children operating it independently of a parent. Kid profile information (a first name, an avatar symbol/color, and reading log entries) is entered by the parent. We do not collect it on our servers, and v1 does not sync it through iCloud. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not share kid profiles or reading log data with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights — though since we don't collect or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our end to disclose.

When you use the weekly summary share sheet, you choose what to send and to which app.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete individual kids or log entries within the app, or uninstall the app entirely. Uninstalling removes Reading Log data from that device. We cannot recover data for you.

Security

Data is protected by your device's operating system and, where applicable, your device passcode/biometrics. Reading Log uses standard Apple APIs (SwiftData) and does not implement a separate Reading Log-operated backup or iCloud sync in v1.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

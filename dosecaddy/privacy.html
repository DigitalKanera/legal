DoseCaddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

DoseCaddy (“the app”) is published by Chad Kanera (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. DoseCaddy is a parent-operated household inventory tool for tracking medicine cabinet items, expiry dates, and a simple dose log. It is not a doctor, pharmacy, or medical device. It does not provide medical advice.

Summary

  • We do not operate user accounts or a server. There is no DoseCaddy account and no cloud sync in v1.
  • We do not sell personal data. The app has no ads or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Household inventory, people labels, locations, and dose-log entries stay on your device (SwiftData).
  • App Store privacy nutrition label: Data Not Collected (accurate for v1 as described here).

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • People — names and optional notes you enter (household member labels, not patient charts).
  • Locations — named storage places (kitchen cabinet, bathroom, travel bag…).
  • Inventory items — name, form, strength text, optional quantity, expiry date, notes, and assignments.
  • Dose log entries — when something was taken, freeform amount text, and optional notes.
  • Settings — reminder preferences and Lifetime Unlock state.

This is local-only v1 (SwiftData on device). We do not transmit any of this data to our own servers, because we don’t operate any.

No account, no cloud

DoseCaddy has no account and no iCloud / CloudKit sync in v1. Reinstalling or switching devices may clear local data unless you exported it yourself.

Notifications

Optional expiry and low-stock reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device using Apple’s standard notification APIs. No push notification server or third-party service is involved. Notification permission is requested only after you continue past the first-launch disclaimer (or when you enable reminders in Settings).

Purchases

DoseCaddy offers a one-time Lifetime Unlock (~$6.99 USD, price may vary by storefront) via Apple StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may collect information according to Apple’s Privacy Policy. The app stores a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support).
  • Health records from Apple Health / HealthKit (DoseCaddy does not use HealthKit).
  • Location from Core Location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics events.

Exports you choose to share

If you use Export (text or PDF), the file is created on your device. Anything you share via the system share sheet goes wherever you send it — that is under your control, not ours.

Children

DoseCaddy is a utilities-style household tool for parents/caregivers. It is not directed at children as a Kids Category app. Do not enter children’s sensitive medical records into freeform notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@digitalkanera.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

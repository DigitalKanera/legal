DoseCaddy

Expiry & dose log. Buy once. Not a doctor or pharmacy.

DoseCaddy is a parent-operated household inventory tool for what’s on your shelf — people, locations, expiry dates, and a simple dose log. Local-first. No subscription.

This app does not provide medical advice. Follow the label and your clinician.

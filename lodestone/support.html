FAQ

Is Lodestone a subscription?

No. Lodestone includes a 14-day full-feature trial. After that, unlock everything with a one-time $2.99 Lifetime Unlock — not a recurring subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Lodestone → Settings → Restore Purchase, or use Restore Purchase on the paywall. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

Does my data sync to the cloud?

Lodestone keeps your focus log and settings on your device (and Apple’s on-device sharing between iPhone and Watch where the app uses it). We do not operate a cloud account or server backup for your history.

Will my trial reset if I reinstall?

Your trial start date is stored with Apple’s on-device persistence (for example Keychain / App Group storage as implemented in the app). Reinstalling on the same device generally preserves your trial window. A new device typically starts a new trial period.

Nightly Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Nightly Minutes (“the app”) is published by Chad Kanera (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. Nightly Minutes is local-first: it works on your device with no Nightly Minutes account and no servers we operate. There is no iCloud sync in 1.0.

Summary

  • We do not operate user accounts or a server. There is nowhere for your reading log to be sent.
  • We do not sell personal data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Reading logs, settings, and purchase unlock state stay on your device. The widget is status-only and reads that same on-device data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • Reading logs — book/title, minutes or pages, an optional note, and a date.
  • Settings — preferences you choose in the app.
  • Unlock state — a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

The Home Screen widget is status-only. It reads the same on-device reading log so you can see progress at a glance. Tap-to-log from the widget is not in 1.0.

We do not transmit any of this data to our own servers, because we don't operate any.

Purchases

Nightly Minutes offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may collect information according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The app stores a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support).
  • Precise location.
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone input.
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.
  • Book catalog or ISBN lookups — titles you enter stay on your device.

The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no social network, and no CSV/PDF export.

Children's privacy

Nightly Minutes is a parent/student productivity reading log, not a Kids Category app. A parent or student uses it on their own device to keep a paper-style reading log. We do not collect reading-log information on our servers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the app is not directed at children operating it independently of a parent.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not share reading logs or settings with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights — though since we don't collect or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our end to disclose.

Weekly share is optional and user-initiated. If you choose to send a weekly summary, iOS presents the system share sheet with text and an image so a parent or student can send it (for example to a teacher). We do not upload that share to our servers.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete individual reading log entries within the app, or uninstall the app entirely. Uninstalling removes Nightly Minutes data from that device. We cannot recover data for you.

Security

Data is protected by your device's operating system and, where applicable, your device passcode/biometrics. Nightly Minutes uses standard Apple APIs and does not implement a separate Nightly Minutes-operated backup. There is no iCloud sync in 1.0.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

FAQ

Is Nightly Minutes a subscription?

No. Nightly Minutes is a one-time $3.99 in-app purchase through Apple — not a recurring subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Nightly Minutes → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

Does my data stay on my device?

Yes. Nightly Minutes is local-first: reading logs, settings, and unlock state stay on the device you're using. There is no Nightly Minutes account, no servers we operate, and no iCloud sync in 1.0. We cannot recover data for you if you delete entries or uninstall the app.

What is weekly share?

Weekly share is optional. When you choose to send a weekly summary, iOS presents the system share sheet with text and an image so a parent or student can send it (for example to a teacher). We do not upload that share to our servers.

What does the widget do?

The Home Screen widget is status-only: it reads the same on-device reading log so you can see progress at a glance. Tap-to-log from the widget is not in 1.0.

FAQ

Is Chorebox a subscription?

No. Chorebox is free to try for 14 days with full feature access. After that, it's a one-time $4.99 purchase — not a recurring subscription. There's no banking or real-money allowance transfer built in; it's a chore chart and points tracker only.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Chorebox → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

Are rewards ever locked behind the paywall?

No — redeeming a reward your kid has already earned points for works during the trial and after it, whether or not you've unlocked the app.

Does my data sync between devices, or to the cloud?

Local-first is the default: kid profiles, chores, and history stay on the iPhone you're using, with no Chorebox account. Optional family sharing (Apple iCloud / CloudKit) can sync that data to invited family devices when you turn it on in Settings. We do not run a Chorebox login or our own cloud.

How many kids can I add?

Up to 4 kid profiles per device.

Can I use a photo instead of an emoji for a kid's avatar?

Yes — in Manage Kids, edit a kid and tap Choose Photo. You can remove it later to go back to the emoji avatar.

What does the parental PIN do?

If enabled in Settings, it requires a PIN before opening Manage Chores, Rewards Catalog, Manage Kids, or Settings — so kids can mark chores done and redeem rewards without being able to edit the rules themselves.

Will my trial reset if I reinstall?

Your trial start date is stored on-device. Reinstalling on the same device generally preserves your trial window. A new device starts a new trial period.

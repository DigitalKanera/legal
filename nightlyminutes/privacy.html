Reading Log: Nightly Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Reading Log: Nightly Minutes (“the app,” “Nightly Minutes”) is published by Chad Kanera (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. Nightly Minutes is a parent-operated K–5 reading log. It is local-first: it works on your device with no account, no iCloud, and no servers we operate.

Summary

  • We do not operate user accounts or a server. There is nowhere for your family's reading log to be sent.
  • We do not sell personal data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Kid profiles and reading entries stay on your device. We do not use iCloud or any cloud sync in this version.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • Kid profiles — a name, an avatar symbol and color, a preferred unit (minutes or pages), and an optional nightly goal.
  • Reading entries — book title, minutes or pages, date, and an optional note entered by the parent.
  • Settings — trial/unlock state, daily reminder time, selected kid, and appearance preference.

Kid profiles, entries, and trial/unlock state live in Apple's shared App Group container (group.com.shiftcrew.readinglog) so the iPhone/iPad app and the Home Screen widget can read the same information. Selected-kid and appearance preferences use Apple's UserDefaults on the device. The app also uses standard file-timestamp APIs as part of local storage (declared in the app's privacy manifest). None of this is sent to us.

We do not transmit any of this data to our own servers, because we don't operate any.

No iCloud, no account

Nightly Minutes does not create an account and does not use Apple iCloud or CloudKit. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device. We cannot recover a reading log for you.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device using Apple's standard notification APIs. No push notification server or third-party service is involved.

Purchases

Nightly Minutes is free to try for 14 days. After that, a one-time in-app purchase (lifetime unlock, $3.99 USD) is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may collect information according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The app stores a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support).
  • Precise location.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone input, or your photo library.
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

Children's privacy

Nightly Minutes is a tool for parents to log K–5 reading homework for their own children, operated by the parent on the parent's own device. Kid profile information (a first name and reading minutes or pages) is entered by the parent. We do not collect it on our servers and do not use iCloud. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the app is not directed at children operating it independently of a parent. Nightly Minutes should be submitted as a parent-operated education utility, not the Kids Category.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not share kid profiles, reading entries, or history with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights — though since we don't collect or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our end to disclose.

When you use the weekly summary share sheet, you choose what to send and to which app.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete individual kids or entries within the app, or uninstall the app entirely. Uninstalling removes Nightly Minutes data from that device. We cannot recover data for you.

Security

Data is protected by your device's operating system and, where applicable, your device passcode/biometrics. Nightly Minutes uses standard Apple APIs (SwiftData, UserDefaults, App Groups) and does not implement a separate Nightly Minutes-operated backup or cloud.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Nightly Minutes Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Nightly Minutes (“the app”) is published by Chad Kanera (“we,” “us”). This policy describes how the app handles information. Nightly Minutes is a parent-operated K–5 reading log, not Kids Category. It is local-only v1 (SwiftData on device): it works on your device with no account, no Chorebox PIN, and no iCloud.

Summary

  • We do not operate user accounts or a server. There is no Nightly Minutes account.
  • We do not sell personal data, and the app contains no ads/analytics SDK.
  • Kid first names/avatars/entries stay on device. Local-only v1 uses SwiftData on device. There is no iCloud sync and no Chorebox PIN.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally to provide its features:

  • Kid profiles — first names and avatars entered by the parent.
  • Reading entries — book titles, minutes or pages, dates, optional notes, and weekly summaries generated on device.
  • Settings — trial/unlock state, reminder time, and reading-unit preferences.

This data is stored with SwiftData on your device (local-only v1). Kid first names/avatars/entries stay on device. We do not transmit any of this data to our own servers, because we don't operate any. There is no iCloud and no account.

No Chorebox PIN, no iCloud, no account

Nightly Minutes has no account, no Chorebox PIN, and no iCloud. Parental controls from Chorebox (including a PIN) are not part of this app. Optional family sharing / CloudKit is not offered in v1.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device using Apple's standard notification APIs. No push notification server or third-party service is involved.

Purchases

Nightly Minutes offers a 14-day trial then $3.99 non-consumable via Apple, processed entirely through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may collect information according to Apple's Privacy Policy. The app stores a local flag when a purchase is verified so the unlock remains available offline.

Data we do not collect

  • Your name, email, or contact information (unless you email us for support).
  • Precise location.
  • Contacts, microphone input, or your full photo library.
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.
  • No ads/analytics SDK.

Children's privacy

Nightly Minutes is a parent-operated K–5 reading log, not Kids Category. It is operated by the parent on the parent's own device. Kid first names/avatars/entries stay on device; they are entered by the parent. We do not collect them on our servers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the app is not directed at children operating it independently of a parent. Submit and list this app as a parent-operated utility, not the Kids Category.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not share kid first names, avatars, or reading entries with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights — though since we don't collect or store your data ourselves, there is nothing on our end to disclose.

When you use the weekly share, you choose what to send using the on-device share sheet.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete individual kids or entries within the app, or uninstall the app entirely. Uninstalling removes Nightly Minutes data from that device. We cannot recover data for you. There is no iCloud copy in v1.

Security

Data is protected by your device's operating system and, where applicable, your device passcode/biometrics. Nightly Minutes uses standard Apple APIs (SwiftData on device) and does not implement a cloud backup. Local-only v1. No iCloud.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

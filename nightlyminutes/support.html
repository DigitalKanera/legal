FAQ

Is Nightly Minutes a subscription?

No. Nightly Minutes is not a subscription. It is free to try for 14 days with full feature access. After that, it's a one-time $3.99 non-consumable via Apple — not a recurring subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Restore in Settings. Open Nightly Minutes → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

What happens when the trial ends?

Adding or editing log entries requires the one-time unlock. Viewing Calendar, History, and Weekly Summary is not gated.

How does weekly share work?

Weekly share is on-device share sheet. You choose the destination (Messages, Mail, Files, and so on). There is no teacher account. We do not send the summary to a server.

Is there a teacher account?

No teacher account. Nightly Minutes is a parent-operated K–5 reading log on your device. Teachers receive a weekly summary only if you share it yourself. Weekly share is on-device share sheet.

Does my data sync between devices, or to the cloud?

No. Local-only v1 (SwiftData on device): kid first names/avatars/entries stay on device. There is no account, no Chorebox PIN, no iCloud.

Will my trial reset if I reinstall?

Your trial start date is stored on-device. Reinstalling on the same device generally preserves your trial window. A new device starts a new trial period.

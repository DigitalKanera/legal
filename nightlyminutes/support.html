FAQ

Is Nightly Minutes a subscription?

No. Reading Log: Nightly Minutes is free to try for 14 days with full feature access. After that, it's a one-time $3.99 purchase — not a recurring subscription. Unlock everything forever. There is no account and no banking.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Nightly Minutes → Settings → Restore Purchases, or use Restore Purchases on the paywall. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

Does my data sync between devices, or to the cloud?

No. Kid profiles and reading entries stay on the device you're using. There is no Nightly Minutes account and no iCloud sync in this version.

What can I log?

Each entry stores a book title plus minutes or pages (you pick a per-kid default unit), the night it was read, and an optional note. Up to 4 kid profiles.

How do I share the week with a teacher?

Open the Week tab and use the share button. That uses Apple's share sheet from your device — we don't host or transmit the summary.

Will my trial reset if I reinstall?

Your trial start date is stored on-device. Reinstalling on the same device generally preserves your trial window. A new device starts a new trial period.
